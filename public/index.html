Welcome! 💜

Thank you for joining the Southville Run For A Cause 2026 — a community fun run organized by Southville for Others (SFO) and the Alumni Department.

This event brings together the entire SGEN community — students, parents, faculty, alumni, friends, and partners — for a day of wellness, unity, and giving back.

All proceeds will support the Pawikan Conservation Center in Bataan and the Bindoy Community in Dumaguete, helping learners with special needs.

Where your registration goes

Every peso raised supports these two communities.

Before you register

Please take a moment to read the reminders below:

  1. Complete all required fields in this form.
  2. Choose your race category and preferred shirt size.
  3. Settle your registration fee by bank transfer, or — for SISC employees — through salary deduction.
  4. Upload your proof of payment before submitting. Employees paying by salary deduction have nothing to upload.
  5. Read and agree to the Waiver of Liability and Health Declaration before final submission. Both open as PDFs:
Waiver of Liability PDF · opens in a new tab Health Declaration PDF · opens in a new tab
Note: Each registration is for one (1) participant only. If you’re registering multiple participants (like family members), please fill out a separate form for each person.
